About Landmark Roofing

A family roofing business in Lingfield

Roofing runs in Robbie’s family: his grandad was a roofer for over 50 years. Today Robbie runs Landmark Roofing from Lingfield with a team of three full-time roofers.

We take on every kind of roofing job, from repairs and guttering to new tiled and slate roofs, flat roofs, chimneys and heritage work, across Surrey, Sussex and Kent.
